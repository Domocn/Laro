Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Introduction

Laro Food (“Laro”, “we”, “our”) runs the website at laro.food, the Android app (package com.laro.app), and related services. We collect what we need to run accounts, recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, and optional AI or health features. We do not sell your personal data or share your recipe library with advertisers.

Information we collect

  • Account: Email address, display name, hashed password, optional Google or GitHub OAuth identifiers, 2FA secrets if you enable them, referral / friend codes.
  • Content you create: recipes, photos, imports (URLs, PDFs, archives), cookbooks, meal plans, shopping lists, pantry items, notes, household and friend relationships, support tickets.
  • AI inputs (optional): prompts, recipe context, PDFs, and images you submit for import or chat, subject to plan quotas.
  • Health (optional): on-device Health Connect nutrition writes stay on your device. If you link Google Health, we store encrypted tokens and may send anonymous nutrition logs when you mark a meal cooked.
  • Automatically: IP address, browser or app version, device type, session metadata, Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, Crashlytics crash reports, and basic Firebase Analytics events on Android (app opens and feature aggregates). We do not use advertising-ID based ad networks.
  • Web storage: local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and record cookie-notice choice.
  • Where visits come from: when you open the website without being signed in, we count where the link came from (for example Instagram, WhatsApp or a promo code), the page and the device type (iPhone, Android or computer). This is stored on our own server, is not linked to you, and is not shared. If you then sign up, that source is saved on your account so we know which links work.

How we use information

  • Provide, sync, and secure the service
  • Run optional AI features you request
  • Send verification, reset, and security email when email is enabled
  • Send push reminders you enable
  • Manage subscriptions and referrals via RevenueCat and Google Play
  • Improve reliability (crash reports) and understand aggregate Android usage

Who we share data with

We share limited data with processors only as needed for the feature you use:

  • OpenPanel (optional first-party web analytics, when enabled: page views only, no ads)
  • OVH (UK hosting) and Cloudflare (DNS, CDN, bot protection)
  • Google Firebase: FCM, Crashlytics, Analytics on Android
  • Google / GitHub for optional OAuth
  • Google Health API if you connect it
  • RevenueCat and Google Play for subscriptions
  • Email delivery (Resend or SMTP)
  • AI providers (cloud models such as Groq / OpenAI-compatible APIs, or Ollama when configured) when you use AI
  • Open Food Facts Open Prices for public cost-catalog sync where enabled

We do not sell personal data. Household members, friends, and public share links only see what you choose to share.

Your recipes

Imported recipes are stored as copies in your account, not as live links that vanish if a blog dies. You can export JSON or a ZIP from Settings at any time, including a GDPR export of all personal data. Self-hosted deployments store data on the operator’s server.

Retention, security, children

We keep account data while the account is active. Deleting your account removes or anonymizes personal data from primary systems within a reasonable period, except limited records required for security, law, or billing. Hosted data is in the United Kingdom; some processors may process data elsewhere. Laro is not directed to children under 13.

HTTPS is used in transit. The Android app uses an encrypted local database for offline use.

Your rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your data from Settings, or email privacy@laro.food. Support: app@laro.food.
