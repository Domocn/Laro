Free plan (honest limits)

The free hosted plan is intentionally small. Unless you are on Laro Pro, a referral trial, or redeemed reward slots, the hosted service is limited to:

  • 50 recipes (up to 500 copies until 15 November 2026 if you are moving off Mealime or another closing app)
  • 3 cookbooks
  • 3 friends
  • 5 recipe shares per week
  • 2 household members (you plus one guest)
  • 3 lifetime AI uses (shown in Settings)

Hitting a limit asks you to upgrade or redeem rewards. We do not delete your existing recipes when you hit the cap.

Laro Pro and auto-renewal

Laro Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play (and web billing where offered). Price is shown on the purchase screen before you pay. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, Google Play charges the price for the next period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Cancel in Google Play subscriptions. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Pro removes the free caps above and unlocks unlimited AI meal plans / imports according to the current paywall. We do not offer a one-time lifetime SKU on Play unless one is listed in the store.

Your recipes are yours

You keep ownership of content you add. Laro stores copies (not fragile bookmarks) so a blog going offline does not wipe your card. You may export JSON or ZIP from Settings at any time. If hosted Laro were ever discontinued, export still exists until the service is turned off; we will give reasonable notice when we can.

Do not import content you do not have the right to store. Public share links are visible to anyone with the link.

AI and health

AI import, chat, and meal plans can be wrong (ingredients, steps, calories). Check before you cook or eat. Nutrition estimates are not medical advice. Microphone access is for on-device hands-free cook commands. Camera access is for cookbook / receipt scans you start.
